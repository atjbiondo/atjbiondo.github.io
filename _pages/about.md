## Background

I studied neuroscience and economics at Bowdoin College. I later worked in litigation support, where my experience has included legal research, discovery, case investigation, and trial preparation. My academic foundation still shapes the areas of law and technology I’m drawn to.

I’m now most interested in privacy, AI, platform governance, and broader questions at the intersection of law, technology, and human behavior. Much of that interest centers on how AI systems, platform design, and data practices influence behavior and decision making, as well as the legal and regulatory questions they raise.
